# DK Seed Bank

[Open the DK Seed Bank website](https://dereksparks1982.github.io/seedrepo/)

318 seed entries · 411 commercial packets · 7 bags · 9 saved seed lots · 19 categories.

Full dictated seed inventory is recorded. Seed packets default to one unless the user specifies otherwise. Bags and saved seeds are recorded separately. Same-species synonym entries are combined: Lemon Mint / Lemon Bergamot (3 packets) and English / True Lavender (6 packets), with original labels preserved in inventory notes. Ambiguous species names require packet verification.

Includes seed planting guides and visible plant-specific Caution reasons, handling instructions and who is at risk; personal journal, photos and garden-workbench data stay in browser storage unless exported.

## Garden Watering Manager

All 318 seed entries show a watering guide in their individual Planting Guide & Details, including when to check soil, in-ground weekly quantities (where applicable), germination, young plants, establishment, flowering or fruiting, containers, drought and overwatering cautions.

Watering Manager is a practical records dashboard, **not a game or a visual garden editor**. It draws from actual Planting Journal entries (not merely seed packets owned). Record a watering, soil-moisture check, rain observation, or skipped watering, with date, measured amount/unit, soil condition and notes. The next date is a **soil-moisture check**, never a guarantee that water is required; frequency can be adjusted per planting and growing setup. Records can be edited or deleted, and exported/imported with the existing complete JSON backup. No push notifications, network weather, automatic synchronization or fabricated watering history.

Watering records are local to the browser (`dk-seedbank-watering-v1`). Existing journal/workbench/photo records are not migrated or reset. Guidance uses general extension advice for typical ground-grown plants; species, soil, rainfall, heat, container size and growth stage determine actual needs.
